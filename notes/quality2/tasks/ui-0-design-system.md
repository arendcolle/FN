# Task UI-0: the design system for this round's pages

## Goal

Two browser walkthroughs found 6 blockers and 18 shoulds on the pages ([research/ux-walk-walk-new.md](../research/ux-walk-walk-new.md), [research/ux-walk-walk-check.md](../research/ux-walk-walk-check.md)). A card has a median of 41.5 words and quotes fill 60% to 79% of a person page ([research/ux-patterns.md](../research/ux-patterns.md)). Decisions U1 to U9 and R4 in [design.md](../design.md) fix that. A page may only use what the design system defines, so this task changes the design system first. It ships no site code. UI-1 is the only site task that copies its result into the site; UI-2 to UI-5 use that copy.

## Where you work

You edit only the design system's working copy, called `ds/` below; the lead gives you its folder. Files: `ds/project/README.md`, `ds/project/components/<Part>/README.md` and `preview.html`, `ds/project/components/bundle.css`, `ds/project/design-system.json` (its `lastChange` note), `ds/STRINGS.md` and `ds/DESIGN-NOTES.md`. First copy the repository's `notes/e2e/design-system/STRINGS.md` and `DESIGN-NOTES.md` over the two in `ds/`: the repository copies are newer (they hold the 20-per-page wording). Leave `ds/preview/` and `ds/site-fn.css` alone. Read `ds/project/README.md` whole before you start.

## Rules that hold for every part

- Reuse tokens and parts first: `fn-back`, `fn-section`, `fn-line`, `fn-lines`, `fn-cite`, `fn-quote`, `fn-note`, `fn-link`, `fn-more`, `fn-new`, `text-*` classes. A new class only where no existing one fits. Every padding uses `space-*`; no font rules outside the `text-*` classes.
- Keep every rule the site uses today. Rules the site stops using after UI-4 (`fn-card__ties`, `fn-card__why`, `fn-fact`) stay in `bundle.css` this round.
- Keep the results page as it looks: card box, list beside map, count line, Show more. The owner likes it.
- Every state below gets a case in its part's `preview.html`, with the caption line the previews already use (`<p class="cap text-small">`). Check each preview at 390 px and 1280 px wide.
- Names in previews are the made-up ones the previews use today, such as Lien Vermeulen and Sofie De Smet.

## Parts to add or change

1. **BackLink (new part, folder `BackLink`).** The one way back at the top of a page opened from another page. Markup: `<p class="fn-back text-meta"><a href="…">Back to the results for “Flemish experts in 6G…”</a></p>`. Labels, word for word: "Back to the results for “`{question cut at 60 characters}`”", "Back to your network", "Back to `{person name}`". No origin known: no back link. Under it, only on a person page opened from a search, the walk line: `<p class="fn-walk text-meta"><span>4 of 143</span> · <a href="…" rel="prev">Previous</a> · <a href="…" rel="next">Next</a></p>`. The first person has no Previous and the last no Next, with their " · " left out. States: results back with walk line in the middle, first, last; back to your network; back to a person; none. Move the back-link line out of the Profile README into this part.
2. **SummaryList (new part, folder `SummaryList`).** A `dl` of key and value rows under the head. Markup: `<dl class="fn-summary-list"><div class="fn-summary-list__row"><dt class="text-label">Studied</dt><dd class="text-body">Ghent University, <span class="text-year">2010–2014</span></dd></div>…</dl>`. Rows in this order: "Studied", "Tie to Flanders", "Contact". An empty row is left out, except Contact, which reads "Not found" in `muted`. Contact holds "`{email}` · `{phone}`". Keys `muted`, between `rule` lines like `fn-contact__row`, stacking under 900 px. States: three rows; Contact "Not found"; Studied left out.
3. **Why block (in Profile).** Only on a person page opened from a search: `<section class="fn-why"><h2 class="fn-section text-label">Why for this search</h2><ul class="fn-why__lines text-body"><li>…</li></ul></section>`. Up to three lines in this order: the card's reason line, the tie in words or "No known tie to Flanders", the place words. A line with nothing to say is left out.
4. **Claim (changed).** A fact is one line: what, organization, years, then the source's short name as a small link that opens the proof in place. Markup: `<div class="fn-claim" id="claim-{id}"><div class="fn-claim__head"><div class="fn-claim__line"><span class="fn-claim__what text-body">{New}Studied at Ghent University, <span class="text-year">2010–2014</span></span> <details class="fn-proof"><summary class="fn-proof__name text-small" aria-label="Source for Studied at Ghent University: ugent.be">ugent.be</summary><div class="fn-proof__body">{quote}<p class="fn-cite text-small"><a href="…">{page title}</a>, March 2024 · <a href="…">Original page</a></p></div></details></div>{Not correct form}</div></div>`. The source name sits on the fact's line where it fits; the proof opens under the line. "Not correct" (`fn-link text-meta`, `muted`) stays at the right of the head, outside `details`, so it never moves when the proof opens. The quote is a Quote of at most 25 words. No date known: the cite ends after the title, never "date unknown". A record has no quote and no Original page link when its url starts with `fn:`. Removed state unchanged: "`{fact}` · Removed" with Undo, in place. States: closed; open with quote; open with the quote left out (title and links only); record; New; Removed.
5. **Quote (changed).** In a proof, at most 25 words cut around the matched words, with "…" at a cut end. A quote that is page debris is left out and the title and links remain. Cards show no quote.
6. **Profile (changed).** Order: back link (and walk line), head, why block, SummaryList, sections, Possible matches. Head: name, role line, then "Combined from `{n}` records" in `fn-profile__combined text-small` `muted` as a link to `#sources`, only for a page opened from a search whose card combines records, then the network state. "In your network since 3 November 2026" with the button "Remove from network". Sections, in order: "Work", "Education", "Ties to Flanders and Belgium", "Places", "Contact" (network people, with Edit, as today), "Sources", "Possible matches". Each fact section shows three facts, then ShowMore "Show `{n}` more". An empty section is left out; Places no longer reads "Not provided". A section whose only facts already stand in the summary is left out. "Found as" becomes "Sources": one row per record and source, its fact count, and "Not this person" on every record but the page's own. States: candidate from a search (why, summary, walk line); network person (Remove from network, Contact); combined from 2 records; after Remove from network (Banner).
7. **PersonCard (changed).** In order: name, role line, the reason line, the second line, the combined line, the actions. Reason line: `<p class="fn-card__reason text-body">Founder and CEO, Fictional Robotics · <a class="fn-card__source" href="…">fictionalrobotics.com</a></p>`, at most 15 words with the source, the source link `muted`. Second line: `<p class="fn-card__fit text-small">Studied at KU Leuven · elsewhere in the US</p>` in `muted`. No quote, no ties line, no "Matches:" line. A card without a reason shows its role line only. The combined line links to the person page at `#sources`. Every card state keeps its look and height rules.
8. **SectionHeading (changed).** On results, the "New" heading becomes up to four headings, each with its count, in this order: "Topic and a tie to Flanders", "Topic, no known tie", "Tie, topic not confirmed", "Other people found". "Other people found" can show whether or not the tie is a must; under a must it holds only people moved down for the role. "In your network" stays first. A heading prints where its first person falls; an empty one is left out. A search stored before this round keeps "New". Same `h2.fn-section` markup, no new class.
9. **SourceText (changed: the readable source page).** In order: BackLink; `h1.fn-source__title text-title` naming the person; one sentence `p.fn-source__lead text-body`: "The page says: `{fact}`." for a page, "The record lists: `{fact}`." for a record, an import or an entry in FN; the passage `p.fn-source__passage text-body` (about 60 words each side of the quote, "…" at a cut end, the quote in `mark`); "Open the original page" as a link in `fn-source__url text-small` (left out for `fn:` urls); then `<details class="fn-source__stored"><summary class="text-meta">Show the stored record</summary><pre class="fn-source__text text-body">…</pre></details>`. A record has no passage. Opened without a fact: the source label as the title and no sentence. States: page with passage, record, import, stored record open.
10. **MapMarker (changed).** Count markers are `<button type="button" class="fn-marker fn-marker--count text-label" aria-label="4 people near Boston">4</button>`, with the focus ring. Enter or Space opens the popup; Esc closes it and returns focus to the marker. Markers that overlap are joined by MapLibre's own clustering into one count marker. The popup of a count marker starts "`{n}` people near `{city}`", or "`{n}` people here" when nobody has a place text. The map opens fitted to the dots of the shown people whose place is inside the asked area, else to all shown people (the `accent` dots), 64 px padding, maximum zoom 11. Single dots stay canvas circles.
11. **Header (rule only).** Search is the current link whenever the address carries a search, the person and source pages included.
